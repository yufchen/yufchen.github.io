I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at **Purdue University**. My work sits at the intersection of computer networks and immersive systems, with a focus on making networked virtual reality more responsive, robust, and visually useful under real-world bandwidth and loss constraints.

Before joining Purdue, I received my Bachelor of Engineering in Electronic Information Engineering from the **University of Science and Technology of China**.

## Research

My research focuses on the efficient delivery of immersive content over wireless networks. I develop cross-layer mechanisms that connect application-level perceptual value with transport and wireless decisions, including progressive VR streaming, adaptive redundancy for multi-user VR, QUIC scheduling, and quality-of-experience-aware routing.
