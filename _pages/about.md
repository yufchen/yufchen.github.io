I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at **Purdue University**. My work sits at the intersection of computer networks and immersive systems: I design cross-layer mechanisms that make networked virtual reality more responsive, robust, and visually useful under real-world bandwidth and loss constraints.

My current research focuses on **networked VR**, **wireless networking**, **transport protocols**, and **quality-of-experience-aware scheduling**. I am especially interested in systems that connect application-level perceptual value with decisions at the transport and wireless layers.

I am interested in research and engineering opportunities in computer networking, distributed and multimedia systems, and performance-oriented systems design.
